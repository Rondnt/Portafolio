# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Two audiences with equal weight:

- **Clients who hire**: owners and managers of clinics, restaurants, gyms and online stores (mostly in Perú) looking for someone to build or run their software. Often non-technical, often on a phone, arriving from WhatsApp, LinkedIn or a shared link. Job: decide quickly whether José can deliver a working system for a business like theirs, then message him.
- **Recruiters and technical leads**: evaluating José as a hire. Often on a laptop, scanning fast. Job: see real production work, the stack behind it, and a way to reach him (LinkedIn, email).

## Product Purpose

Personal portfolio of José Rondón, software engineer. It shows real systems in production (multi-tenant SaaS for dental clinics, restaurants and gyms, an online store, a company landing page, a voice POS on Stellar), the tools he works with, and how to contact him. Success is a visitor reaching out (WhatsApp, email, LinkedIn) after seeing the projects. Bilingual: Spanish and English.

## Brand Personality

Sober, technical, trustworthy. Calm confidence of someone who ships systems that businesses run on every day. The work (screenshots, live demos, the stack) does the persuading; the interface stays out of its way. Voice is direct and plain, first person, no hype.

## Anti-references

- Generic SaaS template: purple gradients, identical icon-heading-text card grids, giant filler metrics, gradient text.
- Anything that reads as a stock theme rather than José's own site.

## Design Principles

1. **Show, don't claim.** Real screenshots, live demos and the actual stack carry the argument; copy stays short.
2. **Two doors, one page.** A business owner on a phone and a recruiter on a laptop must both find the projects and the contact path within seconds.
3. **Calm over loud.** Motion and color support reading; nothing competes with the work.
4. **Production-grade details.** Responsive, fast, accessible and bilingual, because the portfolio is itself proof of the quality he sells.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA: body text contrast ≥ 4.5:1, touch targets ≥ 44px on mobile, full keyboard navigation with visible focus. Every animation has a `prefers-reduced-motion` fallback. Content available in Spanish and English, with the page `lang` kept in sync.
